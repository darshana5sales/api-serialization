Built specifically for API serialization

Designed around the product, batch, labelling and traceability requirements of API and Bulk Drug serialization under G.S.R. 20(E) — Drugs (Amendment) Rules, 2022.

    Designed specifically for API and Bulk Drug serialization workflows and requirements.

  • Beyond the minimum 11 particulars

  • Flexible & customisable

    Configure custom fields, product information and label templates for your requirements.

  • Controlled & traceable

    Role-based controls, approvals, controlled reprinting, serialization records and audit logs.

  • Cloud-based & scalable

    Centralized access, designed to scale with your products, users, workflows and future regulatory needs.
