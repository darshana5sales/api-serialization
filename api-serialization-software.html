How it works

Six steps from batch sheet to scannable pack

Your team enters what it already knows, an authorised user approves it, and the platform does the rest.

1

Log in securely

Company code, username and password. Browser only — nothing installed on the shop floor.

2

Set up your masters, once

Packaging types, product data — GTIN, NDC, pharmacopoeia, storage conditions — and label templates.

3

Create the batch

Batch number, size, manufacturing and expiry or retest dates, label quantity, template. Two minutes of typing.

4

Get it approved

An authorised user approves, rejects or cancels — with a recorded reason. Once approved, the batch locks.

5

Serials generate themselves

GS1 SSCC codes are issued automatically, usually within five to ten minutes, one per container.

6

Print, scan, trace

Print pending labels in bulk, reprint securely when you need to, and every action stays in the audit trail.

What it changes, in practice

  • Full regulatory compliance without re-engineering the line. The change is upstream of printing, so production is untouched.
  • Fewer manual labelling errors. Data is captured once on the product master and validated, rather than retyped per batch.
  • Shorter audit preparation. Compliance reports and audit logs are exportable on demand instead of assembled from records.
  • Scale across products, packaging types and users. Role-based control means adding a site or a line does not mean rebuilding a process.
  • Protection against relabelling. Authenticated reprint means a label cannot quietly be reproduced by someone who should not.
