About URL Aseptic Automation — who we are

Engineering pharmaceutical serialization from software to the production line

URL Aseptic Automation Inc. is an engineering and technology company providing pharmaceutical serialization, industrial barcoding, product authentication, machine vision, automation and traceability solutions.

With 15+ years of pharmaceutical serialization experience and 450+ packaging lines implemented, our expertise combines software, automation, machinery, and production-line integration.

What we do

Engineering, software, machines & automation under one roof

Our strength is bringing software, machinery, engineering, and automation together to deliver connected solutions for pharmaceutical manufacturing.

  • Serialization & track & trace

    Smart Track & Easy Trace enables serialization, aggregation, traceability, product identification, and end-to-end product tracking across the supply chain.

  • Machines & production-line equipment

    Labelling, printing, coding, aggregation, case packing, and end-of-line equipment engineered around pharmaceutical production.

  • Machine vision

    Advanced camera-based solutions for inspection, verification, detection, identification, and quality control across diverse industrial applications.

  • Automation & integration

    PLC, SCADA, controls and third-party equipment integration engineered into complete, connected production-line solutions.

  • Enterprise & L4 integration

    Integration with enterprise and regulatory platforms, including TraceLink, Rfxcel, SAP ATTP, and TraceKey.

  • Software & digital platforms

    Enterprise and connected software solutions supporting pharmaceutical operations, serialization, traceability and production workflows.
