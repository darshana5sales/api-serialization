Who we are

URL Aseptic Automation is one of the leading solution providers to the pharmaceutical industry for industrial barcoding, product authentication and traceability within a compliance framework — built to combat counterfeiting.

With more than twelve years of serialization experience and an installation base of over three hundred, we build against global guidelines including US DSCSA, EU FMD, Russia, China, Uzbekistan, Brazil and Turkey, through L1 to L5 solutions.

We are an engineering company with in-house expertise in software, automation and complete end-of-line solutions. That matters for a specific reason: our products deliver a single-vendor solution, whether the requirement is track and trace, machine vision or automation. What we do

Four capabilities, delivered by one team rather than assembled from four suppliers:

  • Track and trace. Serialization and aggregation software from line level through to enterprise and regulatory reporting.
  • Machine vision. Inspection and verification systems that confirm what was printed is actually readable and correct.
  • Automation. End-of-line equipment — labelling, checkweighing, aggregation — engineered to work with the software rather than alongside it.
  • Cloud platforms. Hosted applications such as APIQR, for manufacturers who need compliance without an IT project.

L1 to L5, explained

Serialization is conventionally described in five levels. Most vendors cover two or three and integrate with partners for the rest. We build all five:

The five levels of pharmaceutical serialization architecture
LevelWhat sits there
L1Device level. Printers, scanners, cameras and the hardware that physically applies and reads a code.
L2Line level. Control of a single packaging line — the software that drives the devices and verifies each pack.
L3Site level. Coordination across all lines in a plant, including serial number management and batch reporting.
L4Enterprise level. Serial number repositories across sites, and the master data that governs them.
L5Regulatory level. Exchange with government and trading-partner systems, in whatever format each jurisdiction demands.

The API Serialization division

APIQR exists because API and bulk drug manufacturers had a specific problem that enterprise serialization platforms were not built for: a hard domestic deadline, a well-defined but detailed set of requirements, and no appetite for a four-week implementation project.

The division works solely with API manufacturers — to meet compliance with the government regulation on mandatory QR codes, to spread awareness of why traceability matters in the API supply chain, to educate on how to comply with global serialization standards, and to keep track of every new guideline as it is issued.

That last point is the one customers tell us matters most. A dedicated team follows each new notification and amendment, so compliance keeps pace without the customer having to chase it.
