Who we are

The stack

L1 to L5, built in one house

Serialization is conventionally described in five levels. Most vendors cover two or three and integrate with partners for the rest. We build all five — which is why there is no integration finger-pointing when a line stops.

L1

Device level

Printers, scanners, cameras and the hardware that physically applies and reads a code.

L2

Line level

Control of a single packaging line — the software that drives the devices and verifies each pack.

L3

Site level

Coordination across all lines in a plant, including serial number management and batch reporting.

L4

Enterprise level

Serial number repositories across sites, and the master data that governs them.

L5

Regulatory level

Exchange with government and trading-partner systems, in whatever format each jurisdiction demands.
