Legal

Privacy policy

How we handle the information you give us through this website.

Draft — requires legal review before launch This is a working draft covering the site as built. India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act applies to the enquiry form, and the final wording should be confirmed by URL Aseptic Automation’s legal adviser, along with the named grievance officer and the confirmed data retention period.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us:

  • Enquiry form data — your name, company, work email, contact number, the subject of your enquiry and any details you add.
  • Technical data — standard web analytics such as pages viewed, approximate location, referring site, browser and device type.

We do not collect special category personal data through this website, and we do not ask for it.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and arrange a demonstration.
  • To send you a document you specifically requested.
  • To understand which pages are useful, so the site can be improved.

We will not add you to a marketing list on the strength of an enquiry alone. If we want to send you anything beyond a reply to what you asked, we will ask you first.

Consent

The enquiry form requires you to tick a consent box before it can be submitted. That consent covers contacting you about the enquiry you made. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us, and we will stop.

Who sees it

Enquiry data goes to the URL Aseptic Automation sales team and is stored in our customer records. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Some processing happens through service providers — website hosting, email delivery and analytics. They act on our instructions and only for the purposes above.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are retained for as long as there is an active commercial conversation, and for a defined period afterwards for legitimate business record-keeping. The exact period is to be confirmed on legal review.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it if it is wrong;
  • delete it, where we have no continuing obligation to keep it;
  • stop using it for a particular purpose.

Email sales@apiqr.in and we will respond.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for basic analytics only. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are set. You can block cookies in your browser without losing access to any part of the site.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, and form submissions are transmitted encrypted. Access to enquiry records is limited to staff who need it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about data we hold, should go to sales@apiqr.in, or to URL Aseptic Automation Inc., Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India.
